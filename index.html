02 / THE APPROACH

Private by design.
Useful by default.

Icarus is a self-hosted personal research and operations project. Its aim is to make local AI practical without surrendering control of personal data or computers.

  • Operator-controlled infrastructure
  • Source-aware document retrieval
  • Permission-aware calendar changes
  • Measured experimentation and improvement
GOOGLE CALENDAR INTEGRATION

Why Calendar access?

The private Icarus installation accesses an owner's Google Calendar only after authorization, to answer schedule questions and perform explicitly approved event changes.

This public website does not contain a Google login or connect visitors to the assistant or its private systems.
